# Code of Conduct

Be respectful, patient and constructive. Critique implementation, not people. Explain technical disagreements with evidence and respect data licenses and privacy.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing and publishing private information are unacceptable.

Report concerns privately to Brajesh Kumar (`brajesh31`). Include context and links, but do not publish private details. The maintainer may remove content, close discussions or restrict participation to protect a respectful project environment.
